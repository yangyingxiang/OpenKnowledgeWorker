Acme Home Lending — Income Verification Overlay

Effective: January 2026 | Version: 4.2 | Supersedes: Version 4.1 (July 2025)

Applies to: All conventional, FHA, and VA mortgage originations processed through AHL

This overlay supplements the Fannie Mae Selling Guide (B3-3 series) and Freddie Mac Guide (Chapter 5306). Where this overlay is stricter than agency guidelines, AHL requirements take precedence. Where this overlay is silent, agency guidelines apply.

1. General Income Requirements

1.1 Income Variance Threshold

When cross-checking income across documents (e.g., W-2 vs. paystub annualized, or year-over-year W-2 comparison), AHL requires documentation of any variance exceeding significant levels. A significant income variance requires a written Letter of Explanation (LOE) from the borrower and may require additional documentation at the underwriter's discretion.

Note: The specific percentage threshold for "significant variance" is determined by the underwriting team lead and may vary by loan program. Consult your team lead for current thresholds.

1.2 Income Continuance

All income used for qualification must have a reasonable expectation of continuance for at least 36 months from the note date. This applies to all income types including base salary, variable income, and other sources.

2. Fixed Base Income

AHL follows FNMA B3-3.3-01 for fixed base income with no additional overlays. The most recent paystub and most recent W-2 are sufficient documentation.

3. Variable Base Income

AHL follows FNMA B3-3.3-01 for variable base income. A minimum 12-month history is required. Both averaging methods (Average Income and Average Hours) are acceptable.

4. Bonus, Overtime, and Commission Income

4.1 History Requirement

AHL requires a minimum two-year history for all variable income types (bonus, overtime, commission, tips). Income received for less than 24 months is not eligible for qualification regardless of offsetting factors.

AHL Override: FNMA allows income with 12+ months of history with positive offsetting factors. AHL does NOT accept this exception — a full 24-month history is required for all variable income.

4.2 Trending Analysis

For variable income trending, AHL uses a weighted recent performance methodology. When the trend is stable or increasing, use the standard 24-month average. When there is a declining trend, the income must be evaluated for stabilization before it can be used.

4.3 Declining Variable Income

If any variable income component shows a year-over-year decline exceeding 20%, the following additional requirements apply:

  • Branch manager sign-off is required before the income can be used
  • The borrower must provide a written explanation for the decline
  • The underwriter must use the lower of: (a) the most recent 12-month average, or (b) the most recent year's total, when calculating qualifying income

4.4 One-Time or Irregular Bonus

Bonus income that has been received only once (single occurrence) is excluded entirely from qualifying income. This aligns with FNMA's 2-year history requirement but AHL makes no exceptions for first-year bonuses regardless of employer documentation.

5. Self-Employment Income

5.1 History Requirement

AHL requires a minimum two-year history of self-employment. The one-year alternative permitted by FNMA (for businesses with 5+ consecutive years) is accepted by AHL only with the following additional documentation:

  • CPA letter confirming business viability and income consistency
  • Borrower letter explaining the nature of the business and transition from prior employment (if applicable)

5.2 Same-Field Exception

For borrowers who transitioned from W-2 employment to self-employment in the same field with less than 2 years of self-employment history, AHL accepts the FNMA same-field exception with the following additional requirements:

  • CPA letter confirming the business is in substantially the same field as prior employment
  • Borrower transition explanation letter
  • Prior employer verification (verbal or written) confirming the borrower's prior role and departure
Note: "Substantially the same field" determination should consider the borrower's actual job duties and industry, not just job title. For example, a Marketing Director transitioning to freelance Marketing Consultant would generally qualify, but a Marketing Director opening a restaurant would not.

5.3 Schedule C Analysis

All self-employment income must be analyzed using Fannie Mae Form 1084 or equivalent cash flow analysis. The qualifying income is the net amount after adding back eligible non-cash expenses (depreciation, amortization) and applying required deductions.

6. RSU and Restricted Stock Income

6.1 AHL Requirements

AHL follows FNMA B3-3.3-07 for RSU income with no additional overlays. Time-based awards require 12-month history; performance-based awards require 24-month history.

6.2 Valuation

Share-based RSU compensation must use the 200-day moving average stock price as specified by FNMA. AHL does not accept alternative valuation methods.

7. Employment Gaps

AHL follows FNMA B3-3.2-02 for employment gaps. Gaps exceeding 30 days within the most recent 24 months require written explanation. The borrower must have been continuously employed for at least 6 months at the current position for income to be used.

AHL Override: FNMA references gaps within the most recent 12 months. AHL extends the lookback period to 24 months for gap documentation requirements.

8. Large Deposits

Any single deposit in a bank statement that exceeds $3,000 and is not identifiable as payroll must be documented with a paper trail (source of funds letter, supporting transfer documentation, or gift letter if applicable).

Note: FNMA's standard threshold for large deposit documentation is typically 50% of qualifying monthly income. AHL applies a flat $3,000 threshold for simplicity and consistency.

9. Gift Funds

Gift funds are accepted per FNMA guidelines with the following AHL additions:

  • Gift letter must be on file before the appraisal is ordered
  • Gift funds must be seasoned (deposited) for at least 15 days before closing
  • Maximum gift amount: 100% of down payment for primary residence; 50% for second homes

10. Audit and Compliance

All income calculations must be fully documented in the loan file with:

  • Source documents cited for each income component
  • Calculation methodology noted (which averaging method, which rule applied)
  • Any exceptions or overrides documented with manager approval
  • Cross-reference to FNMA/Freddie section number for each rule applied
